# SUNY Fredonia patches

These are SUNY Fredonia's official web logos, downloaded unchanged from the university's Brand Logos page:
<https://www.fredonia.edu/about/offices/marketing-and-communications/brand-guidelines/brand-logos>

They belong to the State University of New York at Fredonia and are **not** covered by this repository's CC BY 4.0 license. Do not reuse them outside this tool without permission from Fredonia's Office of Marketing and Communications.

Following the brand guidelines, the patch list marks every logo `"tilt": false`, and `"lines": false`, so the tool keeps them upright and unaltered, with no clothing lines drawn over them. The symbol on its own is left out: the guidelines allow it only alongside the full logo or with Marketing and Communications' approval.

The arched FREDONIA lettering (`fredonia-arch-white.svg`, `fredonia-arch-blue.svg`) is not an official logo: it is the name set in Graduate, an open-licensed collegiate typeface by Eduardo Tunni (SIL Open Font License 1.1), converted to outlines, in the style of campus apparel. The blue is Fredonia blue, PMS 286 (#0033A0). It is kept upright like the logos. The list also includes the tool's question-bubble patches.
